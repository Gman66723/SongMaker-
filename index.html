About Song Maker+

Make melodies and beats by filling in squares. Change instruments, add tracks, and use your own audio files.

Inspired by the playful interface of Chrome Music Lab Song Maker. This project is independently developed and is not affiliated with Google.

Spacebar plays or stops the song. You can save projects as JSON and export your music as WAV.
